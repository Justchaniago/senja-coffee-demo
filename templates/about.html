ABOUT SENJA COFFEE

Crafting exceptional coffee experiences since 2020

Our Story

Founded in 2020, Senja Coffee began with a simple mission: to bring the finest Indonesian coffee beans to coffee lovers around the world. Our name "Senja" means twilight in Indonesian, representing the perfect moment to enjoy a cup of coffee.

We work directly with local farmers across Indonesia's coffee-growing regions, from Sumatra to Java, ensuring fair trade practices and sustainable farming methods. Every bean is carefully selected, roasted to perfection, and packaged with care.

Today, Senja Coffee serves thousands of customers through our cafes and online platform, maintaining our commitment to quality, sustainability, and community.
